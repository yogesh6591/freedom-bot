Sending

Emails go out from the Gmail connected in the chat app. Turn sending on or off here.

Follow-up emails by stage. The chat sends each person the email for where they are; nobody has to write it. Empty a box and save to go back to the standard text. People who are booked with no payment offer yet, have paid, or finished with no result yet get no email. Payment reminders stop after 3 emails, at least 3 days apart.
